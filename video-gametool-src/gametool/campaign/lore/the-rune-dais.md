# The Rune Dais

Three pillars stand around the lectern, each capped with a stone that hums faintly *cyan* at dusk.

![The dais — placeholder sketch](assets/images/placeholder-landscape.svg)

Travelers leave a line of their own story in the book before they move on. Nobody knows who placed the first page.

1. Touch a pillar to hear an old voice.
2. Read the book to hear a new one.
3. Write in it so the next traveler hears yours.
