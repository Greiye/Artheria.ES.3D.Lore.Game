# Tales of Artheria: features (v0.16)

What the game offers today, built and on `main`. Backlog ideas not yet built are left out; see `CHANGELOG.md` for detail.

**Core loop:** the lore lives in the Ruined Library's books. Natalie collects it for a patron; the witch in the ruins keeps the library in flux. Carry books out alive and they count.

## Lore and the library
- Ruined Library open from the start: 12 lore shelves, red books, crumbling ruin, floating stone, rare quiet magic.
- Read lore books in-game; some also read as comics (panels, captions, speech bubbles).
- Pin margin notes to pages; library reading room shows the GitHub wiki.
- Write your own lore (Extras → Write lore) from templates.
- Your works: every piece has Send / Share… to submit it to Mike; text stays on the device until sent.

## Story and quests
- Character creation at new game, then a Natalie cinematic; wake at the camp with her welcome.
- First-meeting cinematic: Magister Greer and Natalie.
- Natalie's missing-party mystery: casebook (J) with clues, theories and her following your lead.
- Casebook quest groups: meet all 16 townsfolk, use all 14 stations (10 crowns each).
- Natalie's patrons send gifts as books reach her (crowns, smith discount, starter kits, scrolls).
- Dialogue with portraits; Natalie, Kavik, Candor, Greer, Lenju, Taitai, the Curio Pedlar and more.

## The descent (roguelike under the library)
- 30 floors, a new layout each run, dragon's hoard at the bottom; stone stairs down from the library floor.
- Permadeath: a fallen hero loses bag, crowns and carried books; writing and art stay. Last words go on the Inn's death tally.
- Retire by bringing the hoard out alive; your drawing joins the survivors' wall.
- Goblins scale by depth: packs, slingers, lurkers, thieves, shamans, captains that learn your habits.
- Every goblin rolled from trait tables, named; rare ones titled. Goblins pick up fallen heroes' gear.
- Named guardian bosses; Natalie ties ropes as shortcuts. Rest camps every 5 floors.
- Flux floors: dark, flooded, burning, or light gravity.
- Witch's scrolls: pick one buff per run (Rime, Witchfire, Thorns, and more).
- Tutorial first descent with Natalie as safety net; 8-minute stairs timer after that.
- Stair cinematics with floor hints; Quick Dungeon node map as no-death practice.
- Deeper rooms pay crowns and lore books; goblin-hunter statue records your best run.

## Combat
- Two hands, any pair: sword, dagger, mace, spear, crossbow, sling, rocks, torch, buckler, round shield.
- Blocking with shields and swords; works against bolts and rocks.
- Crossbow with reload timing, iron sights (Ranger gets a true crosshair); sword combos.
- Gear wears; grindstone teaches sharpening, oiling and mending.
- Kavik spar: 3D fighter rules (four limb buttons, ~25 moves, sidesteps, throws, juggles), talk before and after.

## Professions
- Fighter: Lunge, Sweep, steadying breath.
- Ranger: crosshair, steadier aim.
- Mage: magic missile, taught by Magister Greer.
- Monk: lock on, hand-to-hand duel with goblins.
- Candor's archery range: lessons and scored targets.

## Making things
- Tailor & Barber-Surgeon: profession, name, motivation, secret, backstory, body (strength/endurance), barber, face, outfit, surgeon.
- Weapon forge: crossbow and sword designs (parts, colours, engraving, name, legend), constraint dice.
- Scriptorium sketchbook and writing desk.
- Gallery shows your work and books handed in.

## World
- Aralon: nine-building storybook town, plaza, fountain, river, windmill, market stalls, lanterns, decorations.
- Four exits to their own maps: Aralon Edge, the Beach, Ranlean Gates, Amethyst Peak gates; wagon travel between them.
- Day, sunset and night; sleep at the campfire to move time; torches and lamp light.
- Townsfolk assembled from a morphology spec (body sliders, 50 parts); they walk, spar, haggle, chat.
- Language NPCs: Spanish caravan, Japanese tea house, Tagalog market corner; phrasebook you fill in.
- Portal to a modern flat with Josh, Jason, Liv and Rev.
- Living map (M): fog, "What's here" list, notes anywhere.

## Side games and activities
- Skyrunner: seeded A-to-B glider runs, 1-in-20 dogfight, cockpit view, phone tilt steering.
- Knight frame: pilotable mech (strike, guard, ground slam).
- Campfire: 7-stem band, hear or tell tales, ballads.
- Real skills: knots, first aid, fire craft, cooking recipes, dojo drills, camera push-up counter (opt-in).
- Coin-flip wager at Tam's table; cinema screen; shop with real prices (500 crowns to start).
- Pets: farm dog, cat, owl, hedgehog, seven named dogs, giant hamster; they warn of goblins and sniff out clues.

## Multiplayer
- Friends-only rooms by code, up to 4, peer-to-peer (no server).
- Shared world, looks, pets, preset phrases and emotes, one sky.
- Shared descent: same floor seeds, shared goblins, rejoin friends below; wagon rides carry friends along.

## UI, controls and options
- Keyboard and mouse, full controller support, touch controls with haptics; rebindable keys.
- Three cameras (overhead, shoulder, first person); see-through walls.
- Parchment panels on the right, sized to fit phones; Diablo-style item cards in the bag.
- Low / Medium / High presets plus auto quality; cel-shaded, ink-outlined look.
- English or Español UI.
- What's new, Inspirations, Evolution and Asset credits in Extras; DM view (`?dm`) for map and quest editing.

## Saves
- Three profiles per device, each with its own hero, save, bag and writing.
- Back up everything to one file; restore on any device; JSON export/import per profile.
- Save migrations keep old saves working; the browser is asked to keep data.

## Platform
- Runs in the browser on PC, phone and tablet; no install. Add to Home Screen for fullscreen.
