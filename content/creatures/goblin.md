# Goblin
*Remnant of the Biomancer of Nier*

> Three-foot green pests that steal and breed. Short-lived; said to be dying out.

## Details
- **Size:** Small (about three feet)
- **Lifespan:** About 25 years; adult at five
- **Homeland:** Farmland and caves near human settlements
- **Appearance:** Green skin, quick hands
- **Society:** Breeds fast; little knowledge passed down

## Abilities
- **Nimble**: Goblins are not strong, enduring or clever, but they are quick on their feet.
- **Improviser**: Creative with whatever they pick up as a weapon.

## Lore
Goblins are the remnants of the **Biomancer of Nier**, who reshaped the races around his kingdom to forge one of his own. They were his soldier strain, made to wear an enemy down through sheer numbers. See [[farm-beasts]].

Ethical biomancers estimate that goblins will die out within eight generations (two hundred years).

**Sources disagree.** Human folk say goblins are failed orc births. Quimzy the Orc says they are orcs who were cast out. *Farm Beasts* says the Biomancer made them. Derby's teachers blame a Mad Tinkerwright.
