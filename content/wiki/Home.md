# Tales of Artheria

A small 3D lore game built on **Artheria**, a world made by a group of friends over years of tabletop play.

**[▶ Play the game](https://greiye.github.io/Artheria.ES.3D.Lore.Game/)**

## The premise

The lore lives in books that can only be found in the **Ruined Library**. **Natalie Bourbon**, explorer-cartographer, is collecting it for a patron. A **hag witch** haunts the ruins and stands in the way.

## How to read the lore

Artheria's history is told by many voices, and they don't agree. Where they clash, a page says **⚖ Sources disagree** and names who says what. No version is final.
