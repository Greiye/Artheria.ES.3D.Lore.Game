# Kingdom Lore: The Red Kingdoms and the Azul Empire

*Part of [[History]] · from* Tales of Artheria

**Before the Red Kingdoms**, many kingdoms, each with its own king, warred for land and resources.

**The Azul Empire.** A cut-throat businesswoman discovered a resource and monopolised it at once, using her contacts to amass an enormous fortune and with it an army: the **Azulan Army**. She merged two towns into one city in half a year, work that should have taken decades. Then she besieged and conquered one kingdom after another, once taking a walled castle in 48 hours.

**The Candlewater Conflict.** Faced with new, unknown technology, the kings set aside their differences and banded together as the **Red Kingdoms**. The war lasted a few years. Her empire was growing faster than it could hold, and a ceasefire was called. She abandoned some conquered kingdoms to fend for themselves and stayed to build up her first two towns, now one city called **Topaz**; its two halves are the **Crown** and the **Culet**. The ceasefire holds today, and many believe it is only a matter of time before the Azulan Army returns.

**The Empress's secrecy.** Her name became a secret during her rise. Rumour says she had every noble family that knew it hanged; other rumours say power drove her mad and she changes her name daily. Everyone simply calls her **the Empress**.

**The Cavalry.** Every Azulan war machine is accompanied or operated by spellcasters who hide it from sight. No one knows what the machines look like, only that they are machines. Low-ranking soldiers knew them only as "the Cavalry". Wherever the Cavalry is heard, there are rainstorms and fog, darkness by day, and Azulan soldiers struck blind, deaf, or both. Field medics were told to avoid it in every scenario.
