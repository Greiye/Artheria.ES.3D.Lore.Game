# Chronology

*Part of [[History]] · from* Tales of Artheria

*Built from the World above. ⚖ marks dates the sources dispute.*

## 1. The First World (undated, pre-migration)

1. **Titans of Creation**: universe made by titans; world may be a cell in a titan. (Elven scripture)
2. **Celestial Conflict**: titans war for millennia; their blood forms seas; life sparks from clashing god-swords.
3. **Titanic Spirits**: spirits shape land; ancestors of all races, basis of religions.
4. **Changelings**: first sentients (proto-man), shapeshift but not resize. ⚖ Veilo S3 note: changelings "super rare, ~50 exist", lore update pending.
5. **Beastmen**: changelings fixed in animal form; worship the Rabbit God + totem. ⚖ Summary says "Rabbit God", section heading says "Fox God".
6. **Giants**: claim descent from last titan couple; 6 fingers; stone age.
7. **Elves**: descended from peaceful spirits; innate magic; ~1000-yr lifespan.
8. **Fairies**: 4-winged, use ambient mana; elf–fairy friendship births healing magic.
9. **The First War**: Giants vs Beastmen; Beastmen enslave elves (living shields), grind fairies for potions. Many generations.
10. **Pendragon Lineage**: elf Pendragon frees elves/fairies, becomes first king; Elven-Fairy Collective (magitech) vs Beastman-Giant Aggregate. Pollution spawns corrupted spirits. War ~**800 yrs**. ⚖ "Pendragon" also names the later *human* Scholar (see 2.8); text says the Scholar "traced lineage to Pendragon" and is "known as Pendragon".
11. **Home Among the Stars**: Collective poisons oceans, flees dying world by starship; Aggregate follows. Centuries in transit; Aggregate reduced to one ship, **the Ark**. Fey (last pure spirits) stow away.

## 2. Artheria (Caerne continent)

1. **Landing**: Collective lands, names planet **Artheria**; meets Halflings (Gnomes/Dwarves, bronze age). Native threats: Grand Oculon, western dragons, roaming elementals.
2. **+Decades**: Giants & Beastmen land. Great War resumes.
3. **Halfling legends** (undated): Cold North (Lich Queen Aureya, lost halfling city), Northern Waters, Unexplored East + Chalice, Eastern lights, Western scaled monster (later slain by Kazka), Roaming Elementals.

## Era of Ice: Y -2000 → 2000 (4000 yrs)
⚖ Ice end date: header gives Y2000; Iceless Frontier section is headed **Y1980**; Kazka's section is headed **Y2000** and says his party "would have been impossible during the Era of Ice", so Y2000 = post-Ice. Treat end as ~Y1980–2000.

4. **Frozen Struggle**: races ally to destroy the Ark → **Giants extinct**. All races fight for food; ancient tech lost; elf–halfling peace ends; fairies dwindle. ⚖ Giants appear later: King Breitling (12 ft Giant), toddler giant (S15 plan), Frost Giant boss (Sins of the Father), Titankins in parties.
5. **Search for the Chalice of the Gods**: explorers seek godblood chalice said to end the Ice. Ruins of all races date from here.
6. **Humans**: round-eared, short-lived elves born; no magic; cast out (copper age). With Halflings, build stone society, discover electricity, found the **Library of the Wise**.
   - **Church of the First** founded (Ein; magic via collective faith).
   - Humans found **Nepenthe** (island).
7. **Birth of the Orcs**: humans use fairy "Ash Magic" → Orcs; cast out. ⚖ Alt version: orcs were enslaved labor that rebelled.
   - **Orcish Confederation / Oxen Union**: 6 colonies (House of Humies, Greyskins, Rockraiders, Bloodflags, Ochre Ogres, Steelmills), 13 Notes; later weakens, reform ("2 Choice Room"). (Inserted by Mike, IRL 2024-01-04)
8. **Human–Orc War / The First Pact**: orcs burn the Library of the Wise (→ **Ruined Library**). The **Scholar** (Pendragon line) summons **The Patron** for warmth + elven power.
   - **"The Promise that Shattered the World"**: portals open, demons released, gray-smoke sky. Emberkin Portal + Noctavel Portal (beneficial). Necromancy introduced. Webwraiths (corrupted elves) arise.
   - Scholar = **First Warlock**; war ends; he vanishes 9 months later. His children = **first Infernlings**; found underground town.
9. **Nepenthe / Ephemerals** (during Ice; follows Nepenthe founding): Arresters enslave Nepenthe seeking the **Seed of Thithonus**. Lumari slaves **Anarch, Vellichor, Petrichor** drink sap of the Evergreen Sapling, leap 100 ft cliff → immortal "Ephemerals".
10. **Anarch founds Amethyst Peak** ("thousands of years ago, during the Era of Ice"). Tames dragons; first sighting of Dragons; names the **Elder Dragons of Corvathe**.
    - ⚖ "Amethyst Peak Settled in the Year **1000**" (inside Ice era) vs Iceless Frontier: "Ranlean, Aralon and Amethyst Peak were founded" *after* Ice ended.
    - ⚖ Anarch described both as a deity and as a Lumari ex-slave from Nepenthe; compatible if he's an immortal Ephemeral later deified.
    - **AP Historic Stages**: Village (cave → dragon nest, bandits enslaved, amethyst mine) → Town (castle, fire elementals tamed, dragon riders, dragonglass found, trade with Ranlean/Aralon, Anarch kills a talking dragon) → City (**"1100 years had passed"**; Dragonglass town; 3 wall layers; 10 dragon drops; Anarch legendary).
    - Black Bay attack fails (closest call: Black Bay destroys AP mining op). "Winged Threat Era" of random dragon raids (undated).
    - **~500 yrs before present**: acid-breathing dragon recorded. Rampant Dragon terrorizes AP for 500 yrs (undated start).

## Iceless Frontier: ~Y1980–2000 →
11. Chalice reportedly found, purifies world, Ice ends. Era of relative peace. All races trade on Caerne. **Tria Prima** (Ranlean, Aralon, Amethyst Peak) formed; Medieval Age.
12. **Adventurer's Guild** formed. ⚖ Two origins: (a) post-Ice community-building org; (b) born from the later **Infernling–Orc War** as mercenary call. Guild now seeks the Chalice in **Aevelon**; expeditions to other continents never return.
13. **Return of War** ("centuries later"): orc bloodline degenerates → **Goblins**; orc society fractures to raider tribes; intelligent bloodline becomes Orc nobility. **Infernling–Orc War**. ⚖ Goblin origin conflict: degenerate orcs (History) vs Biomancer of Nier's engineered soldiers (book "Farm Beasts", Appendix).
14. **Lostia** (east): centuries of war, never won or lost (book "Eastern Affairs").
