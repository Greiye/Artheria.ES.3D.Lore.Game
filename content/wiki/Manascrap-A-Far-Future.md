# Manascrap: A Far Future

*Part of [[Tales and Campaigns]] · from* Tales of Artheria

*One telling of what Artheria might become.* The races of Artheria banded together and reached peace, with planes, super-yachts, building-sized mechanical suits and telescopes that saw other worlds. Then someone pulled the atomic trigger. Eighty years later, animals and monsters live alongside the people in the sprawl. The city spreads 70 miles and goes a mile underground, its decaying industry overgrown with vines, giant robots standing forgotten in the mist, and people using technology they no longer understand.

Magic is still around, but no one uses it. Whatever killed the people and plants killed the magic too: it is toxic, smells, and comes out like gunk. A firebolt may also spit flecks of rust that cut the caster and leave an infection. Everyone's mana veins are clogged, so even the old magic doors no longer open. The real magic now belongs to the **Technomancers**, the **Mages of the Script**. The original code mages died at the moment of impact, and the handful left know how to open the sealed vaults of non-radioactive material, protected by magically dense metals. They hide, and they keep a code.
