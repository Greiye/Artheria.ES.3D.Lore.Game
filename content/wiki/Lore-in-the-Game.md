# Lore in the Game

**[▶ Play](https://greiye.github.io/Artheria.ES.3D.Lore.Game/)** · [About](https://greiye.github.io/Artheria.ES.3D.Lore.Game/about/)

- You arrive by stagecoach at a storybook town beside **the Ruined Library**.
- **Natalie Bourbon** waits at her camp near the ruins. She is collecting lore for a patron, a friend of **Magister Greer**. Her party, **the Archer, the Mage and the Swordsman**, is missing.
- **The Ruined Library**, once the Library of the Wise. Goblins have moved in. A **hag witch** keeps it in flux: a 30-floor descent where books only count once you carry them out to Natalie.
- **The lorebooks** the hag hoarded: *Yellow Blood*, *Scales of the Shore*, *Friar Birel Lialdon: Life of an Elven Follower*, *Eastern Affairs: War of the East*, and *Farm Beasts: The Goblin Plague*.
- **Kavik Zamir** stands by the dojo.

## Not yet told
Some things are still undecided, so the game leaves them open:

- Whether her ruin is the Library of the Wise itself.
- Why Natalie chose this site.
