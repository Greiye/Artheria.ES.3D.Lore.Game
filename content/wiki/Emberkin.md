# Emberkin

*Part of [[Peoples]] · from* Tales of Artheria

Emberkin are fire people who constantly radiate heat. Most have orange hair and ashen skin and otherwise appear human. They can control heat in general, which makes them akin to sorcerers. They all came from a portal which has since disappeared, and so they are a rare sight: most people know them only from stories.

> ⚖ **Sources disagree.** *Jenna* and the Emberkin she grew up with say their portal is gone. The *Royal Chronicle* says Amethyst Peak’s Royal Fire Brigade still guards the Emberkin Portal. Either the Brigade guards a dead rift for prestige, or Jenna’s family never saw it.

**Pyroveil** is the fabric Emberkin use to reflect the heat their bodies radiate. It can be layered to multiply its effect, and may come from the Emberkin Plane they came from.
