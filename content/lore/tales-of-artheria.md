# Tales of Artheria: A Foreword

*Collected by Natalie Bourbon, Artheria's Explorer, Cartographer and Historian Extraordinaire.*

What follows is everything I have gathered: my own notes, tavern hearsay, and whatever friends and strangers write in this book. Where my sources disagree, I let them. Choosing one teller over another is a job for whoever comes after me.

## The seven continents

The world of Artheria is split into seven continents. Each keeps its own ecosystem.

- **Silverpeak**, the Winterlands. Always frozen. Giant woolly beasts roam there, and sailors name it for a mountain so tall it cuts through the fog.
- **The Hymlie Principality**. Taiga and desert, and the richest source of magic. It holds more books than anywhere else, which is why I live here.
- **Starhaven**. Irradiated swamps behind a caustic fog. Sailors see millions of lights through it, like a sky full of stars. No one goes in or out.
- **Sylvena**. Grassy flatlands. A colony was lost there; it left behind writings about beasts and strange humanoids.
- **Northern Emeris**. Deciduous forest kept in line by giant golems of metal.
- **Central Emeris**. Rain forest, home to dead things that still walk. A colony lasted a decade there, then gave up.
- **Southern Emeris**. People whose tools far outstrip ours, speaking a language that is hard to learn.

## Why "Principality"?

The legend says a prince ruled the land for eons but never became king, because a king must first sip from the Golden Chalice, and he never found it.

**Sources disagree.** Sailors' maps, and my own notes, call this whole land the Hymlie Principality. Talar the Human and the sages of Ranlean insist the continent's elder name is **Caerne**.
