# Natalie's Field Note

Field note. The Ruined Library, at the door. Dusk.

The orcs burned this place long ago, and it still hasn't finished
burning. You can smell it in the stone.

Four of us went in at noon. I came out alone. The others are somewhere
between the shelves, and I am out here writing, because writing is the
only thing my hands will do without shaking.

What I know: one goblin. Small. Quick. It doesn't fight like a soldier.
It fights like a child who found a knife and made up a game. It bit
through our scout's crossbow string.

What I don't know: why it stays. Goblins don't keep anything. This one
keeps the red books.

If you are reading this, you are about to go in. Stay low. Leave the red
ones be until it looks away.

I will finish this note when I find them.
