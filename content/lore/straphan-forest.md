# Straphan Forest

You are standing in it. These woods belong to the **Fauna God**, whose spirit walks Straphan Forest in the image of a white deer. Beasts and giants are its creatures.

Travellers who keep still at sunset sometimes see it at the treeline. Nobody has seen it come any closer.
