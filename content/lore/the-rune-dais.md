# The Rune Dais

Three pillars stand around the lectern, each capped with a stone that hums faintly *cyan* at dusk.

![The dais — placeholder sketch](assets/images/placeholder-landscape.svg)

The stones glow the colour of raw, unshaped magic: **blue**. Magic drawn from a god shines white or yellow, a demon's red or orange, a necromantic god's purple or black, and an older nature god's green, brown or blue.

Magic is still largely unknown, even to those who study it. About one in ten people in the world can cast at all, and anyone who pushes past their limit suffers **mana burn**.

Travellers leave a line of their own story in the book before they move on. Nobody knows who placed the first page.

1. Touch a pillar to hear an old voice.
2. Read the book to hear a new one.
3. Write in it so the next traveller hears yours.
