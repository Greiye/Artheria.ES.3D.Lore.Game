# Eastern Affairs: War of the East (or lack thereof)

*The Infernling/Orc War.*

The Kingdom of Lostia has fought for centuries. At one point or another it was at war with every society around it. For all its ferocity and its enduring metal war machines, Lostia has neither won nor lost a single war, because of endless infighting. Its deserts are full of abandoned machinery.

In small raiding groups, the Lostian orcs are frighteningly efficient. They use whatever technology and weapons come to hand.

Stick to the coast and you should be fine. The orcs take great care never to tangle with the water serpents that plague their shores.
