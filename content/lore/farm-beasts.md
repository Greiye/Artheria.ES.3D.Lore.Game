# Farm Beasts: The Goblin Plague

This book covers goblins and the pestilence they bring.

Goblins are the remnants of the **Biomancer of Nier**, who reshaped the races around his kingdom to forge one of his own. Goblins were his soldier strain, made to wear an enemy down through sheer numbers. As a side effect they multiply quickly and reach adulthood in only five years.

They live about twenty-five years, so whatever they learn is poorly passed down. Goblins are not strong, enduring or clever. But they are nimble, and creative with whatever they pick up as a weapon.

Ethical biomancers estimate that goblins will die out within eight generations (two hundred years).

**Sources disagree.** Human folk say goblins are failed orc births. Quimzy the Orc says they are orcs who were cast out. This book says the Biomancer made them. Nobody has checked its arithmetic.
