# Scales of the Shore

*A small picture book: an informational on the Blue Water Dragon.*

Deep in the oceans of the world lives the Blue Water Dragon, a fearsome creature blamed for many lost merchant ships, mostly on carelessly planned routes. They are very aggressive and attack anything they see. They live along the shores, though some have been found as far as thirty miles inland.

Not to be confused with Sea Dragons: Blue Water Dragons have wings.

- Immune to poison. Cannot be exhausted, paralysed or held by magic.
- Sense their surroundings without sight, out to sixty feet.
- Can quench any magical flame we know of.
- Breathe a salt spray that freezes on contact, or a steam spray that scalds out to sixty feet.
- Create, destroy or command water at will.

Coastal folk call the greatest of them **Bakunawa**.

**Sources disagree.** The scholars of Amethyst Peak write only that dragons "may breathe ice". The coastal books, this one included, are certain. The scholars have never read them.
