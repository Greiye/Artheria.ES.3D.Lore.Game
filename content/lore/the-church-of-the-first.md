# The Church of the First

Long ago there was a pantheon of gods: white-winged spirits bathing in golden flame, guarded by swirling rings of gold and marble. At first they cared for the few thinking creatures of the world. In time they grew tired of watching their children make the same mistakes. They began to travel down in pairs, wearing mortal forms and playing with mortals like toys.

One god, **Ein**, said this was wrong. The others attacked him, dragged him down to the mortal realm, and took his rings of protection. They would change their ways, they said, if he could find his way home alone, a feat no single god had ever managed.

Without his rings Ein could be hurt. With the help of new friends and a demon turned ally, he crossed storms, cold snaps, heat waves and wars to reach the tallest peak in the land. There he showed his six-winged form and flew for home.

He flew for years. His wings molted and broke. When he reached the gates, the gods kept their word, for a few centuries. Ein chose to come back down, finding those below more godlike than his kin. Before he left he broke the Gods' Bath and seeded the clouds with thorns. Below, he hid his rings, stowed the last of his flame in a brazier, and died a mortal among his friends. One by one, the other gods fell to the world after him.

> "It is not where we're from or where we're going, but how we get there, and the help we get along the way. Be the First. For you may not be the last."

**Sources disagree.**

- *What colour is Ein's flame?* Church scripture says gold. Friar Birel Lialdon's western sect says blue.
- *God or demigod?* The Church says god. Birel says demigod.
- *Hero or betrayer?* The Church tells of Ein who freed the gods. The House of Basalt tells of Eyan, who poisoned them. It may be one event told from opposite sides.
