# Yellow Blood: Necromancy

*A journal by Beihorn Duskrest, an elf who dabbled in dark revival magic. Found on a library shelf.*

The journal records a necromancer's practice, from small animals upward, testing how damaged a creature could be before the spell failed.

> "Specimens should be healed before they're resurrected. Will this scale to humanoids?"

Later chapters follow the author as they grow older, keeping the work secret from friends and family.

> "It was something like a game, hearing the rumours about graverobbers in the cemetery for the third time this year. If they ever caught me, I'd be hanged. The ones I raised are moving around, but they're aimless. No kind of head activity. Can I make them do chores?"

Then the blunders.

> "Entry 26 (the words are messily scrawled). One of them bit me. I fine-tuned the spell to make them smarter. I guess the part of the brain for hunger came back too. Bit off three of my left-hand fingers."

The book ends there. Tucked inside the back cover is a scrap of a scroll: a minute, lesser spell to animate the dead for twenty seconds.

**Sources disagree.** The Scroll of Infernling Origin and the Church of the First both say necromancy came from the Patron. Sailors say it comes from Central Emeris. This journal proves at least one elf practised it at home.
