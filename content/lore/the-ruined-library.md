# The Ruined Library

Long ago the elves began to bear children with rounded ears, who lived short lives and could not use magic. These first humans were cast out. With the help of the halflings they built a society of stone, and in that age they found something they took for this world's own magic. Its discovery led to the founding of the **Library of the Wise**.

**Sources disagree.** Halfling legends say humans and halflings discovered lightning together and kept it in jars. The ancient elven scriptures say the outcasts were still beating copper at the time, and that anything clever in those years was halfling work.

## The burning

The orcs, cast out in their turn from human society, came back in great numbers and in great anger. War followed, and they burned the Library of the Wise. It has been called the **Ruined Library** ever since.

Some say an artifact from the Noctavel Portal still lies somewhere inside. Nobody tends the place now. There is no caretaker, only scorched stone, fallen shelves, and whatever has moved in.
