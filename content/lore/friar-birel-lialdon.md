# Friar Birel Lialdon: Life of an Elven Follower

This autobiography follows one follower of the Church of the First on a pilgrimage west, along the trail of the demigod Ein. Birel walks past the places where Ein lit the signal fires that kept the western cities safe from raids, and past the southern waters where Ein fought his fallen brothers and sisters. He keeps walking until the city of Lyalondeshe, where he finally grows tired of travel.

Some say Ein himself visited Birel there and gave him part of his blue flame, his soul. The flame was set in a giant brazier at the centre of the basilica Birel founded. It is said to survive any wind. However small the great flame shrinks, its core stays constant.
