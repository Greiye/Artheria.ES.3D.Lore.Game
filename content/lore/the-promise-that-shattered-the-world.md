# The Promise that Shattered the World

When the orcs burned the Library of the Wise and the war turned against humankind, a human wizard known as **the Scholar** delved into forbidden fairy lore. With his wealth and the last of that knowledge he summoned a being from another world: **the Patron**, which spoke from the shadows in empathetic whispers.

The Patron came from a realm of endless agony, where the only language was *destroy or be destroyed*. It spoke in emotions rather than words. It shifted shape at will, a dark mass threaded with green, purple and red, with horns and tails.

## The pact

The Scholar bound it in ethereal chains until they could understand each other. Then they struck a bargain: the Patron's power to save humanity, and its realm's invisible fire to warm human homes through the Era of Ice. In return the Scholar would keep the gates open, so the Patron could stay.

At least, that is how the Scholar understood it. The Patron did not trust so small a creature with so much power, and it deceived him. The price of keeping the gates open cursed the Scholar's bloodline for generations.

The sky went grey. The orcish war ended at once, the orcs overrun by a swarm of lesser demons. When the orcs gave up, the demons wanted to stay. Nobody knows how the Scholar banished them, but he did, and two portals were left behind: the **Noctavel Portal** and the **Emberkin Portal**. What became of the Patron is unknown.

**Sources disagree.**

- *Was the Scholar Pendragon?* The Scroll of Infernling Origin says he was only of Pendragon's line. Talar the Human simply calls him Pendragon.
- *How many portals?* The Scroll says only two remain. Common folk, and my own notes, say portals opened everywhere.
- *What was the grey sky?* The Scholar's own words call it spirits from the world before this one. The Church of the First calls it lesser demons.
