# Ranlean Rhambutan

One of the rarer fruits of Ranlean, and it grows nowhere else: blue, and covered in long, hair-like growth.
The Nackle serves it as a dessert.

**To eat one:** pinch the hairy skin until it splits, peel it back, and eat the pale flesh around the seed.
Don't eat the seed.
