# The Stagecoach

A tan, bald, overweight giant of a man who rules the low-magic frontier.

![The Stagecoach — placeholder art](assets/images/stagecoach-placeholder.svg)

## What people say

- He **never** walks; the ground moves out of his way.
- His coat is stitched from *seven* stolen flags.
- No one has seen him eat, yet every larder he passes is empty.

## Notes for the DM

He carries high kinetic momentum and heavy armor plating. Once he starts moving, he does not stop for walls, wagons or people.
